Keep your escape safe

Progress & puzzles

Where is my progress saved?

Campaign progress, stars, coins, lives, current puzzles, Undo history, cosmetics and Time Rush records are stored on your device. The current game does not require an account or provide developer-operated cloud synchronisation.

Keep the app installed while investigating missing progress. An update is intended to preserve saves; deleting the app can remove them. Your Apple device-backup settings may affect what can be recovered.

I’m stuck. What can I try?

Look at each piece’s direction and match it to its exit. When valid history is available, Undo is free. Restart and Hint controls explain their availability or coin cost before you use them. A No Escape warning means the current board needs recovery; it does not mean you should reinstall the game.

Can I keep playing after completing a level with no lives?

Yes. A successful campaign completion saves its reward and allows forward progression to the next level, even at zero lives. A genuine failed attempt or another retry may still be subject to lives restrictions.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No game account is required. Core puzzles and local Time Rush records work offline. Optional purchases, purchase restoration and advertisements require a connection when those services are available.

Your in-game items

Coins, lives & collections

How do lives refill?

The maximum is five lives. A missing life refills every 36 minutes, and the game shows the next refill countdown. Keep your device’s date and time set correctly. If the count looks wrong, close and reopen the app and check again.

My coins, lives or boosters look wrong.

Stop spending items until you have checked the saved balance. Close and reopen the app once, then note the level, approximate time and what happened. If the problem continues, contact support with those details and a screenshot that hides personal information.

Please do not delete the app, repeatedly buy the same item, or change the device clock to troubleshoot a balance.

Where can I find an owned cosmetic?

Open Shop → Collections, or Profile → Earned Styles. These use the same inventory. Some styles are earned through gameplay; planned premium purchases are separate from earned rewards. If a purchased permanent collection is missing, use the restoration guidance below where supported.

Why is an advert unavailable?

Rewarded adverts are planned, but production advertising is not enabled in the verified pre-release game. In a future supported release, availability can depend on connection, region, privacy choices and provider inventory. Loading, closing or failing an advert does not itself earn a life.

Apple purchases

Purchases & restoration

Pre-release availability

Real-money purchases are not enabled in the currently verified pre-release game. The instructions below apply only when Apple purchases and restoration are supported in your released version.

How do I restore a permanent collection?
  1. Check that you are using the same Apple Account used for the original purchase.
  2. Connect to the internet and open Sort Escape.
  3. Open Shop → Collections → Restore Purchases, where available.
  4. Allow Apple to refresh ownership, then check your collection inventory.

Restoration is for eligible permanent, non-consumable purchases. It does not restore campaign progress or recreate spent coins. If unavailable, keep your existing app and contact support.

Can Restore Purchases bring back coins?

Coin packs are consumable purchases. Apple’s restoration feature does not refill spent consumables, restore a previous wallet balance or replace local game saves. If Apple charged you but a pack was not delivered, keep the app installed and contact support. Do not buy it again as a troubleshooting step.

A purchase is pending, missing or refunded.

A pending Apple purchase may be waiting for approval. Do not repeatedly retry it. If charged but not delivered, note the item and approximate purchase time. Support can help investigate, but cannot promise a refund or ask you for your Apple Account password.

For Apple’s own guidance, see help with missing in-app purchases or Apple’s purchase help.

Try the simple things first

Sound, display & reopening

The game is frozen or will not open.
  1. Close the app normally and reopen it.
  2. If needed, restart your iPhone or iPad.
  3. Check available device storage and install an official App Store update if one is available.
  4. If the issue continues, record the app version, iOS version and the steps that led to it.

Avoid deleting or reinstalling the app while investigating. Local progress may be lost, and the developer cannot retrieve a save that was never uploaded.

I cannot hear music or effects.

Check the device volume and audio output, then open the game’s Settings. Music and Effects have separate switches and volume controls. Also check Musical Moves and whether audio is going to Bluetooth headphones. Close and reopen the app after an interrupted audio session if needed.

Can I make the game easier to read or reduce movement?

In Settings, look for Reduced Motion, colour-matching symbols and Large Direction Arrows. Music, effects and haptic settings can be adjusted separately. If something remains difficult to use, include your device model and the affected screen when contacting support.
