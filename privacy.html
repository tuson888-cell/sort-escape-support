The short version

  • Game progress and Time Rush records stay on your device.
  • No game account is required.
  • Planned Apple purchases and Google adverts involve their own data handling.
  • You choose what to send when contacting support.

This policy covers the game and this support website. The pre-release game currently has purchases and production advertising disabled.

Who is responsible?

Garry Tuson, an independent developer based in the United Kingdom, is responsible for the game’s developer-controlled personal information. Sort Escape is a game name, not a separate registered company.

For support or privacy requests: sortescape@gmail.com

Your progress and local game data

The game stores progress and settings on your device: campaign completions and stars, current puzzles and Undo history, coins, lives and refill times, boosters, achievements, owned and equipped cosmetics, and Time Rush records. An optional profile name and selected avatar are also stored locally.

No game account or sign-in is required. The reviewed game does not upload gameplay to a developer-operated server or provide developer cloud synchronisation. Time Rush rankings are on-device records, not an online or Game Center leaderboard.

Apple or other device-backup services may include app data according to your settings. Their policies govern those backups. An update is intended to preserve saves; deleting the app or its data can remove local progress. We cannot recover information that was never sent to us.

Apple purchases and restoration

In-app purchases are planned but are disabled in the verified pre-release game. If enabled in a later release, Apple handles payment and its Apple Account information. The game uses verified product and transaction identifiers, purchase/ownership status and refund or revocation information to deliver the purchased item and prevent duplicate delivery. The developer does not receive your full payment-card details.

Eligible permanent cosmetic purchases can be refreshed through Apple’s restoration service where supported. Consumable coins and local campaign progress have different recovery rules. Apple retains and handles its own payment records under the Apple privacy policy.

Advertising and Google services

The game includes Google Mobile Ads and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP), which manages advertising privacy choices. Real adverts and in-app purchases are disabled in the reviewed pre-release version 1.0.0, Build 76. Official test adverts have been tested separately; this does not mean live advertising is active.

If advertising is enabled in a future release, checking consent information or loading a privacy message can contact Google before you make an advertising choice and before any advert is requested or displayed. Google may process your IP address, app and device information, and consent-related information to determine which message applies, deliver it and manage your choices. This communication is part of the consent service; it does not itself mean you have agreed to advertising.

Advertising services can also begin processing information during SDK initialisation, before an explicit advert request. The prepared flow completes its consent check and any required privacy message, and checks that Google’s consent service permits an advert request, before starting the advertising SDK or loading an advert. Information can therefore be processed even when no advert has been requested or shown.

When advertising services operate, Google and any configured advertising partners may process IP addresses and approximate location, device or advertising identifiers, ad views and interactions, and crash, diagnostic and performance information. Uses include delivering and measuring adverts, preventing abuse and maintaining the service. Processing depends on the released configuration, region and choices.

The prepared advert requests are non-personalised and publisher first-party ID is disabled. Non-personalised does not mean no information is processed. Google’s privacy policy and Mobile Ads data disclosure explain its practices.

Consent, tracking and your choices

Where advertising is enabled, the prepared flow refreshes consent information before starting the advertising SDK and requesting adverts. That refresh can involve the consent-service communication described above. Applicable privacy messages collect choices, and advertising privacy options appear in Settings when required and available so you can review or change them. Core gameplay does not require a successful advert.

The reviewed game does not request App Tracking Transparency authorisation. Before any future release performs tracking as defined by Apple, the required permission and refusal handling must be in place. Regional advertising consent and Apple’s tracking permission are different requirements; one does not replace the other.

You can also manage device permissions, diagnostics and backup choices in Apple’s settings. This website has no advertising or consent SDK of its own.

Diagnostics, analytics and sharing

No Firebase, Sentry, custom developer analytics service or developer crash-upload server is integrated in the reviewed release. Apple may provide App Store, device or TestFlight diagnostics according to your settings and Apple’s policies. Google’s SDKs may process their own diagnostics when their services are used.

Local commerce diagnostics record operation stage and error codes. The game does not deliberately log a payment-card number, Apple Account password or raw signed purchase token in those diagnostics.

Friend challenge and result features let you copy information you choose to share. The reviewed challenge codes describe the puzzle course, not a player account. If you send copied information through another service, that recipient and service receive what you send. The game does not access your address book or provide hosted chat.

If you contact support

You can contact Garry Tuson at sortescape@gmail.com for support or privacy questions. If you email us, we receive your email address, your message and any attachments you choose to provide. We use this information to answer your enquiry, investigate problems and handle related privacy requests.

Please send only what is needed. Hide unrelated personal information in screenshots and never send passwords, full payment-card details, tax records or a complete device backup.

Support email is provided through Google Gmail. Google processes messages and related service information under its privacy policy. Sending an email opens your chosen email service; the website has no contact form.

We rely on our legitimate interests in helping players and maintaining the game to handle ordinary support enquiries, subject to your rights and interests. Where particular records or requests must be handled by law, we rely on the relevant legal obligation. Where consent is required for an optional service, it must be obtained before that processing begins.

This support website

The pages themselves use no analytics scripts, advertising, contact form, tracking pixels or third-party embeds. Fonts and images are served with the site; no external font service is contacted. The site does not set its own cookies or use browser local storage.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. See GitHub’s privacy statement. This does not mean the developer receives an individual visitor log or that GitHub processes no information.

External links, including Apple, Google and your email service, have their own privacy practices when you choose to open them.

Retention, deletion and international processing

Game records remain on your device until removed through app or device controls. Deleting the app may delete local saves; offloading an app is different and may keep its data. Device backups and Apple purchase records can remain separately. Please seek support before deleting anything if you want to preserve progress.

We delete or anonymise routine support correspondence 12 months after case closure or the last substantive contact, whichever is later. We remove unnecessary attachments and unrelated personal information sooner.

If a genuine legal obligation or dispute requires particular information to be retained longer, we keep only what is necessary for that reason, review the continuing need, and delete or anonymise it when the reason ends. You can ask us to review or delete developer-held information about you; we will explain any justified exception.

Apple, Google (including Gmail), and the website host have their own retention and backup practices for information they process. They may process information outside the UK; their privacy notices explain their arrangements and applicable safeguards. Removing a local game save or asking the developer to delete an email does not automatically delete provider-controlled backups, Apple purchase records or advertising-provider records.

Children and families

The game does not require an account or ask for a date of birth. We do not infer a player’s age from their device. A parent or guardian can help a younger player contact support and can email sortescape@gmail.com about a child’s privacy or developer-held support information.

Please avoid sending unnecessary personal information about a child. If we become aware that a support message contains personal information about a child that we do not need, we will review it and remove that information where appropriate.

Production advertising is not currently enabled. Before introducing it, we will assess the game’s intended and likely audience, applicable children’s privacy requirements, and the required child-directed, under-age and consent settings. An App Store age rating or a non-personalised advertising request does not, by itself, establish that these requirements have been met.

Privacy rights and questions

Depending on the information, applicable law and circumstances, you may have rights to access or correct personal information, request deletion or restriction, object to certain uses, or obtain a portable copy. Where processing relies on consent, you can withdraw it for future processing. Local-only game data is not held in a developer cloud account.

Use the approved privacy contact to make a request. We may need enough information to identify and understand your request, but will not ask for your Apple password or full card details. Apple, Google or other providers may need to handle requests about information under their control.

You can also raise a concern with the UK Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint. The ICO explains its own information handling in its privacy notice.

Changes to this policy

This policy must be reviewed before enabling advertising, purchases or any new data service. An updated version will be posted here with its effective date, and additional notice or consent will be used where required.

For game help, visit Support. You can also return to the home page.
